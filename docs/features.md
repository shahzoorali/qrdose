# QRdose — Product Features

**The simple way to let the people who care know you're okay.**

---

## What Is QRdose?

QRdose is a notification service built around a single idea: one tap should be enough to tell everyone who matters that something important just happened.

You set it up once. After that, a single tap of a card — or a scan of a QR code — sends a text message to everyone on your list. No typing. No calling. No app to open.

It is designed for daily routines where consistency and simplicity matter most.

---

## Who Is It For?

QRdose is built for anyone who checks in with loved ones on a regular schedule — and for the families and caregivers who want that reassurance without the back-and-forth.

**Common uses include:**

- Confirming a daily medication has been taken
- Letting family know you've arrived home safely
- A morning check-in for elderly relatives living alone
- Post-workout or post-appointment "I'm fine" messages
- Any routine where the same reassuring message is sent repeatedly

## Features at a Glance

---

### 1. One-Tap Notification

**What it is:** Tap a card or scan a QR code with any smartphone camera and your contacts are notified — no app required.

**Why it matters:** The person sending the notification does not need to navigate a phone, find a contact, or type anything. One physical action is all it takes.

**Key details:**
- Works with any smartphone camera (iPhone or Android)
- Works with NFC-enabled tap cards
- No app installation required for the sender or the recipients
- A confirmation button prevents accidental sends

---

### 2. Custom Notification Message

**What it is:** You write the one message that all your contacts will receive every time the card is tapped.

**Why it matters:** The message is personal and specific — far more meaningful than a generic alert.

**Key details:**
- Written in plain language by the account holder (e.g. *"Murt has taken his medication"*)
- The time of the tap is automatically added to every message (e.g. *"…at 2:34 PM."*)
- The message can be updated at any time through the web portal
- Up to 280 characters

---

### 3. Up to 10 Contacts

**What it is:** Store up to 10 people who will receive the notification every time the card is used.

**Why it matters:** One tap reaches the whole family — children, siblings, caregivers, doctors — all at once, with no extra effort.

**Key details:**
- Each contact needs only a name and a US phone number
- All contacts receive the message simultaneously
- Contacts can be added, edited, or removed at any time
- No action is required from the contact — they simply receive a text

---

### 4. Instant SMS Delivery

**What it is:** Notifications are delivered as standard text messages (SMS) to US phone numbers.

**Why it matters:** SMS reaches everyone. It does not require a smartphone, a data plan, or any particular app. A basic mobile phone is enough to receive the message.

**Key details:**
- Delivered to any US (+1) phone number
- No app or account required to receive the message
- Messages arrive within seconds of the tap
- Works even on non-smartphone devices

---

### 5. Trigger History

**What it is:** A log of every time the card has been tapped, visible in the web portal.

**Why it matters:** Caregivers and family members can review the history at any time — confirming patterns, spotting missed days, or providing peace of mind.

**Key details:**
- Displays the date and time of every successful notification
- Shows how many contacts were reached each time
- Available at any time through the portal
- Sorted with the most recent notifications first

---

### 6. Web Portal

**What it is:** A secure website where the account holder manages their entire QRdose setup — contacts, message, card, history, and billing.

**Why it matters:** Everything is in one place. Updates take seconds and take effect immediately.

**Portal sections include:**

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | See your current message, contact count, and recent activity at a glance |
| **Contacts** | Add, edit, or remove any of your 10 contacts |
| **Message** | Update the notification message sent to your contacts |
| **Card** | View your QR code, get the NFC link, or regenerate your card |
| **History** | Browse the full log of past notifications |
| **Billing** | Manage your subscription |

---

### 7. QR Code + NFC Card

**What it is:** Every account gets both a scannable QR code and a programmable NFC card link — two ways to trigger the same notification.

**Why it matters:** Different situations call for different approaches. The QR code can be printed and stuck anywhere. The NFC card works with a simple tap, no scanning needed.

**Key details:**
- QR code: printable, display on any surface, works with any camera app
- NFC card: a tap from an NFC-enabled phone triggers the notification instantly
- Both are tied to the same account and send the same message
- The QR code is available as a download directly from the portal

---

### 8. Card Regeneration

**What it is:** If a card is lost, stolen, or compromised, the account holder can generate a brand new card with one click. The old card is disabled immediately.

**Why it matters:** Security and peace of mind. No one can use a lost card to send notifications once it has been deactivated.

**Key details:**
- Old card stops working the moment a new one is generated
- New QR code and NFC link are available instantly
- No support call required — done entirely in the portal

---

### 9. Confirmation Screen

**What it is:** When the card is tapped or the QR code is scanned, a confirmation screen appears on the phone before any messages are sent.

**Why it matters:** Prevents accidental notifications from pocket taps, bumps, or link previews loading automatically on shared devices.

**How it works:**
1. The phone screen shows the notification message
2. A button labelled **"Notify my contacts"** must be tapped to send
3. After sending, the screen confirms: **"Your contacts have been notified."**

---

### 10. Personal Confirmation Receipt

**What it is:** After a successful notification, the account holder receives their own copy of the message as a text confirmation.

**Why it matters:** Provides a personal record that the notification was sent successfully, without needing to check the portal.

---

### 11. Duplicate Send Protection

**What it is:** After a card is successfully used, a short waiting period prevents the same card from sending again within 60 seconds.

**Why it matters:** Prevents contacts from receiving the same message multiple times in quick succession due to accidental double-taps or a card being passed between people.

## Security & Privacy

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Private card links** | Every card has a unique, randomly generated link that cannot be guessed by outsiders |
| **Secure account login** | Email and password authentication |
| **Password protection** | Passwords are stored securely and never readable |
| **Immediate card deactivation** | Regenerating a card instantly disables the old one |
| **US-only** | All phone numbers and services are US-based |

---

## What Is Not Included

QRdose is intentionally focused. The following are outside the scope of the product:

| Not included | Reason |
|---|---|
| Location or GPS tracking | Privacy by design — QRdose is notification-only |
| Email notifications | SMS reaches everyone without requiring an email account |
| Mobile app | The tap/scan flow requires no app installation |
| Scheduled or reminder messages | All notifications are triggered manually |
| More than 10 contacts | Designed for close personal networks |
| International phone numbers | US market only at this time |

QRdose removes every barrier between a person and the reassurance their loved ones need.
